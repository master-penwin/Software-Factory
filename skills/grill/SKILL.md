description: Interview the user until README.md is a complete spec.
---
Ask one question per message. Each question states its purpose (the decision it unlocks) and your recommended answer.

Ask the most decisive question first. Usual order: what and who, core mechanic, flow, platform, v1 scope (what "done" means), look.

Write each answer into README.md right away, one line each.

Stop when what's left is cheap to change. Decide those yourself and say what you decided.
